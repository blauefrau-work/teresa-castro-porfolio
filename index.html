01 · Social media collaboration · 2025

Infopédia × Netflix: The Wednesday the Dictionary Didn't See Coming

  • Content & partnerships
  • Instagram · TikTok · X
  • No paid media
  • Shortlist, Creativity Club Portugal Festival
Result10,600+organic likes on the main Instagram post, above its initial target

Outcome

Infopédia updated its dictionary entry for “quarta-feira” (Wednesday) with a new meaning, turning the idea into a visible result. The campaign generated thousands of comments and was amplified by fans, cast members, influencers and brands.

Challenge and approach

  1. Challenge

    Go beyond standard promotion for the new season of Rabo de Peixe: build anticipation, involve fans in the story, and turn their participation into a tangible update to Infopédia's dictionary.

  2. Approach

    A participatory, social-first campaign. A poll asked what residents of Rabo de Peixe are called, a carousel collected Azorean expressions from the series, and a challenge mobilised fans around the dictionary update. Showing the entry being updated live on video made the change feel immediate and shareable. The campaign culminated in a formal petition and a community celebration.

My role

From planning through content development and approvals, with the creative, social and partner teams.

Netflix wanted an update to the entry for “quarta-feira”. I proposed the live-update video, the poll and the carousel of Azorean words.

Context

Rabo de Peixe is a Netflix series set in the village of the same name, in the Azores. One of its characters, nicknamed “Quarta-feira” (Wednesday), gave the word a new meaning.

02 · E-learning content localisation · 2019

Escola Virtual Angola: localising e-learning for Angolan schools

  • Team leadership & project management
  • Team of 8
  • 4 grade levels
  • Agile/Scrum
Result21courses delivered for 1st, 7th, 8th and 9th grades

Outcome

21 localised courses for Angolan schools, for 1st, 7th, 8th and 9th grades, plus a documented, repeatable workflow that shortened onboarding for new team members.

Challenge and approach

  1. Challenge

    Deliver consistent, curriculum-based content across grades and subjects while coordinating authors working asynchronously with content managers and designers working in real time. The materials had to feel native to Angolan students, not translated or reused.

  2. Approach

    The key decision was to fully localise. Content was adapted to the Angolan curriculum and to Angolan Portuguese, written and spoken, and audio was re-recorded with Angolan speakers. I ran the lifecycle with Agile/Scrum planning and tracking, documented workflows for handoffs and onboarding, and ran quality checks before each release.

My role

Team Leader and Project Manager, from initiation to closure, coordinating authors, content managers and designers.

Context

Porto Editora's e-learning platform for the Angolan market. In 2019 Angola recorded about 6.4 million students in grades 1–9 (Ministry of Education of Angola / INE, Statistical Yearbook of Education 2019).

Skills

Content localisation, linguistic and curriculum adaptation, cross-functional coordination, process documentation, quality assurance.

03 · Corporate website rebuild · 2026

Bloco Gráfico: replacing a legacy website

  • Operations & stakeholder lead
  • Sustainability section
  • 360° virtual tour
  • Reported to the Board
ResultJan 2026launch of a mobile-friendly site that is easier to update

Outcome

The new website of Bloco Gráfico, the printing unit of Grupo Porto Editora, has an interactive virtual tour that lets teachers and students see how books are made. It also has refreshed videos and a sustainability section covering environmental, social and governance commitments, organised around the ISO 14001-certified environmental management system.

Challenge and approach

  1. Challenge

    The legacy site wasn't mobile-friendly, its design and videos were outdated, and it was hard to update. It had no sustainability section and didn't show the production facility in an engaging way. Replacing it required cross-department agreement and content approved by the owner of each area.

  2. Approach

    I translated business needs into a clear site structure and priorities, and brought departments together to agree what to publish and who owned each piece. Securing sustainability information took months of follow-up and, when needed, executive involvement to unblock delivery. I kept the project on schedule and monitored the site in Google Analytics after launch.

My role

Managed project operations from requirements through launch, aligned departments, and coordinated developers and designers.

Context

Bloco Gráfico, S.A. has been Grupo Porto Editora's printing and finishing division since 1944, based in Maia.

Its sustainability page reports a 22% lower carbon footprint (2021–2024), more than 90% of waste sent for recovery, and about 20% of energy produced for self-consumption (2024). These are the company’s results, not mine. My part was the website that shares them, and I’m proud to work for a company that does this.

Skills

Stakeholder management, content governance, sustainability communication, in-house CMS, Google Analytics.

04 · Portuguese as a foreign language · 2017–2018

Português Mais Perto: courses for different levels and audiences

  • Team leadership & project management
  • Team of 8
  • Partnership with Camões, I.P.
  • CEFR A1–B2
Result6courses pairing proven material with new discovery-based grammar activities

Outcome

Six courses of Portuguese as a foreign language: A1 and A2 for children and young people, A1 to B2 for adults. We built the first with Camões, I.P. and then extended to adult beginners on our own.

Challenge and approach

  1. Challenge

    One set of courses for very different learners, from beginner to upper-intermediate, each matched to its level and audience. The courses had to go beyond explanatory videos and engage learners actively.

  2. Approach

    Combine reuse with targeted new creation. We reused material that already worked and created new activities where they added most, leading learners to discover the grammar rule themselves. Content was written by authors specialising in Portuguese as a foreign language and adapted to each CEFR level and age group.

My role

Team Leader and Project Manager, coordinating specialist authors, content managers and designers from initiation to closure.

Context

Porto Editora's platform for learners of Portuguese abroad. In 2017, 68,758 students were learning Portuguese in primary and secondary education abroad (Camões, I.P., reported by Lusa/DN, 21 September 2018).

Skills

Instructional design, pedagogical adaptation by level and audience, content curation and reuse, partnership management.

05 · Story-led English-learning resource · 2010–2011

Choose Your Path: a branching story for English learners

  • Storyboard & narrative
  • Interaction architecture
  • Development
  • Internal creativity contest
Result3rd / 12teams in Porto Editora's internal creativity contest

Outcome

A non-linear resource for teaching English, developing listening, reading, writing and speaking. It is designed for replay: each choice is another path to explore, never a dead end.

Challenge and approach

  1. Challenge

    An open brief, a fixed timeframe and 11 competing teams, judged on creativity, accuracy, educational value and technical execution. The idea had to raise the bar for interactivity and give learners a reason to come back.

  2. Approach

    A branching story in which each choice opens a different route. I mapped scenes, branches and the points where paths rejoin so every route stayed coherent, narratively and pedagogically. Drawing on my experience as an English teacher, I brought reading, listening, writing and speaking into one experience where learners work out language rules for themselves.

My role

Content Manager in a two-person team with a designer. I wrote the storyboard and narrative and built the branching architecture in the authoring tool.

I also commissioned a linguistic review by a native English speaker and supervised the voice recording for pronunciation, pacing and tone.

Skills

Narrative design, non-linear interaction architecture, instructional design, linguistic quality assurance.

The resource is internal and confidential, so it is not shown.
